About This Project

The main purpose of this School Management System is to provide a simple and organized way to manage school data. Instead of keeping teacher and student information in separate files or manually maintaining records, the system stores and manages the information through a centralized database.

The project allows administrators to add, view, edit, and delete records, making school data easier to manage and maintain.
